The Sun

The blazing heart of it all.

What is the Sun?

The Sun is a yellow dwarf star at the center of our Solar System, holding 99.86% of its total mass. Driven by nuclear fusion in its core, it generates the extreme heat, light and radiation that sustain life on Earth, and its gravity holds every other body in orbit.

Why is space interesting?

Exploring space gives humanity a deeper understanding of our origins, the fundamental laws of physics, and the possibilities of life beyond our home planet. Studying celestial environments inspires new technologies, drives scientific innovation, and reminds us how unique and fragile Earth really is.
